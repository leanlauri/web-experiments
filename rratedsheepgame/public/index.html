A LITTLE MISCHIEF. A VERY BIG DREAM.

Your guide to the Moon.

Walk near animals to invite them along. First bring all twelve of your sheep home. Then recruit animals from the six colorful farms. You can lead only one species per trip.

WASD / arrows move relative to the camera; Shift runs; Space jumps. E boards a nearby vehicle or gets out. The driver takes one seat; each animal takes another. Get close to your herd before boarding. Extra animals follow on foot.

Vehicles use the same camera-relative directional controls as walking: point the joystick or press WASD / arrows toward your destination. The car turns toward that direction, while momentum carries it through the turn. Release to coast. Hold Space / Brake for a quick stop. Sharp turns and hard braking can break traction, leaving tire marks that fade away.

Vehicles collide with other vehicles. Fast impacts send people and animals tumbling, with stars circling their heads as they recover. Bullets hit your vehicle hull instead of its occupants. Below 20% hull health, a vehicle burns for five seconds, then explodes. Exit quickly: blasts damage and launch nearby cars and characters. Traffic victims wake automatically; a wake-up snack can revive an animal sooner.

F uses your weapon, aiming at the nearest opponent in range; Q opens the equipment wheel: hold, point and release to choose. On touch, tap Items then an item. Watch for friendly fire! R gives a nearby unconscious animal a wake-up snack. B opens your home shop. M opens the map. Scroll to zoom.

Rivers divide the outer farms. Use the town streets to travel between counties. Farm gates face town. Borrowing animals or vehicles alerts police. Stay more than 33 m from all awake police for 12 seconds to lose one wanted star. Repeat until clear. Home prevents attacks, but you must still escape the police search.

Pause at home for four seconds to settle a trip. Animals inside the home circle or awake in your vehicle count. All other animals return to their original farm. Every successful trip makes guards stronger and more numerous, and later narrows farm gates.

Start with three hearts. A knockout costs one; losing all means five seconds in jail, then a ride home without your herd. Home refills hearts. Buy five rocket parts with cumulative animal credit, then launch! Progress saves locally at home.
