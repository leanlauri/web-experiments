A LITTLE MISCHIEF. A VERY BIG DREAM.

Your guide to the Moon.

Walk near animals to invite them along. First bring all twelve of your sheep home. Then recruit animals from the six colorful farms. You can lead only one species per trip.

WASD / arrows move relative to the camera; Shift runs; Space jumps. E boards a nearby vehicle or gets out. The driver takes one seat; each animal takes another. Get close to your herd before boarding. Extra animals follow on foot.

In a vehicle, W / up accelerates, S / down brakes then reverses, and A / D steers. On touch, use the joystick up/down for the pedals and left/right for steering. Hold Space / Brake for a quick stop. Vehicles coast when released, turn only while moving, and slow down on grass. Buggies turn tightly; vans and buses need more room.

F uses your weapon, aiming at the nearest opponent in range; Q changes weapons. Watch for friendly fire! R gives a nearby unconscious animal a wake-up snack. B opens your home shop. M opens the map. Scroll to zoom.

Road bridges cross rivers. Farm gates face town. Borrowing animals or vehicles alerts police. Stay more than 33 m from all awake police for 12 seconds to lose one wanted star. Repeat until clear. Home prevents attacks, but you must still escape the police search.

Pause at home for four seconds to settle a trip. Animals inside the home circle or awake in your vehicle count. All other animals return to their original farm. Every successful trip makes guards stronger and more numerous, and later narrows farm gates.

Start with three hearts. A knockout costs one; losing all means five seconds in jail, then a ride home without your herd. Home refills hearts. Buy five rocket parts with cumulative animal credit, then launch! Progress saves locally at home.
